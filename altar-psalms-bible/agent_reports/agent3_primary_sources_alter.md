# Primary Text Sources for Esoteric/Working Use — Psalms

## 1. Robert Alter — *The Hebrew Bible: A Translation with Commentary* (W.W. Norton, 2018-19)

The complete three-volume set; Psalms sits inside **Volume 3: The Writings (Ketuvim)**. Alter strips away the King James pulpit cadence and gives back the Hebrew's terse rhythm, concrete imagery, and refusal of body-soul dualism — the right tool for working that needs the original sound and image, not Victorian smoothing.

**Where to buy:**
- Publisher (W.W. Norton) — full set, ISBN 9780393292497: https://wwnorton.com/books/9780393292497
- Amazon hardcover three-volume slipcase: https://www.amazon.com/Hebrew-Bible-Translation-Commentary-Three/dp/0393292495
- Kindle ebook (single combined edition): https://www.amazon.com/Hebrew-Bible-Translation-Commentary-ebook/dp/B07BN5HWWX
- Audible audiobook, 64 hours, narrated by Edoardo Ballerini, Recorded Books 2019: https://www.amazon.com/The-Hebrew-Bible-Robert-Alter-audiobook/dp/B082BZYFJY
- Standalone *Book of Psalms* paperback (2007 edition, ISBN 9780393337044) — cheaper entry point if you want only the Psalms: https://www.amazon.com/Book-Psalms-Translation-Commentary/dp/0393337049 and Norton's page: https://wwnorton.com/books/The-Book-of-Psalms

**Sample text — Psalm 23 (Alter, 2007):**
> The LORD is my shepherd, I shall not want. In grass meadows He makes me lie down, by quiet waters guides me. My life He brings back. He leads me on pathways of justice for His name's sake. Though I walk in the vale of death's shadow, I fear no harm, for You are with me. Your rod and Your staff — it is they that console me. You set out a table before me in the face of my foes. You moisten my head with oil, my cup overflows. Let but goodness and kindness pursue me all the days of my life. And I shall dwell in the house of the LORD for many long days.

Note "**My life He brings back**" rather than "He restores my soul" — Alter rejects the imported Greek body/soul split; *nephesh* is the living breath. "**Moisten my head with oil**" instead of "anoint" — sensual, present-tense, kitchen-real. Source excerpt: https://www.sdmorrison.org/psalms-23-robert-alters-brilliant-translation/

**Sample text — Psalm 91 (Alter), opening:**
> He who dwells in the Most High's shelter, in the shadow of Shaddai lies at night. I say of the LORD, "My refuge and bastion, my God in whom I trust." … For He will save you from the fowler's snare, from the disastrous plague.

"**Lies at night**" for *yitlonan* (root *l-w-n*, to lodge overnight) — Alter restores the literal sleeping-in-God's-shadow image the KJV's "abide" abstracts away. Reception discussion: https://disjectamembra2014.blogspot.com/2016/12/robert-alter-book-of-psalms.html and the Jewish Review of Books symposium: https://jewishreviewofbooks.com/articles/4901/robert-alters-bible-a-symposium/ — also the Jewish Book Council review: https://www.jewishbookcouncil.org/book/the-hebrew-bible-a-translation-with-commentary

## 2. The Septuagint (LXX) in English

The Greek text the early Church and the Hellenistic Jewish world prayed from. Numbering differs from the Masoretic by one for most of the Psalter (LXX Ps 90 = MT Ps 91; LXX Ps 22 = MT Ps 23).

**NETS — New English Translation of the Septuagint (Oxford, 2007/2009)** — official free electronic edition hosted by University of Pennsylvania CCAT:
- Project home: http://ccat.sas.upenn.edu/nets/
- Full edition page (all books as PDFs): https://ccat.sas.upenn.edu/nets/edition/
- **Psalms PDF (includes both LXX Ps 90 = MT 91 and LXX Ps 22 = MT 23):** https://ccat.sas.upenn.edu/nets/edition/24-ps-nets.pdf
- Internet Archive scan of the Oxford print edition: https://archive.org/details/lxxnets

**Brenton's 1851 Septuagint** — the classic English LXX, still in active liturgical and esoteric circulation:
- LXX Ps 90 (= MT 91) at eBible.org: https://ebible.org/eng-Brenton/PSA090.htm — opens *"He that dwells in the help of the Highest, shall sojourn under the shelter of the God of heaven … He shall give his angels charge concerning thee, to keep thee in all thy ways … I will satisfy him with length of days, and shew him my salvation."*
- LXX Ps 22 (= MT 23) at BibleHub: https://biblehub.com/sep/psalms/22.htm — *"The Lord tends me as a shepherd, and I shall want nothing. In a place of green grass, there he has made me dwell …"*
- Full Brenton index: https://ebible.org/eng-Brenton/ and CCEL: https://ccel.org/b/brenton/lxx/htm/TOC.htm

**The Orthodox Study Bible (Thomas Nelson, 2008)** — Old Testament is the St. Athanasius Academy Septuagint (SAAS), NKJV-base adjusted to LXX, Rahlfs critical text, with patristic study notes. The standard LXX-Psalter in current Orthodox liturgical English:
- Publisher: https://www.thomasnelsonbibles.com/product/orthodox-study-bible/
- Wikipedia overview of edition and translation method: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthodox_Study_Bible

## 4. Dead Sea Scrolls — 11Q5, the Great Psalms Scroll

The Leon Levy Dead Sea Scrolls Digital Library (Israel Antiquities Authority) hosts high-resolution multispectral images of the actual scroll. 11Q5 contains 48 psalms including seven not in the Masoretic Psalter — the longer wisdom psalm 154, Psalm 155, the David's Compositions colophon, an apocryphal Psalm 151.

- Project portal: https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive
- 11Q5 manuscript record (first plate): https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/manuscript/11Q5-1?locale=en_US
- Second plate: https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/manuscript/11Q5-2?locale=en_US
- English transcription/translation companion: https://dssenglishbible.com/scroll11Q5.htm
- Scholarly summary and column-by-column contents: https://three-things.ca/11q5/ and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Psalms_Scroll

## 5. Geneva Bible 1599 (Psalm 91 + Psalm 23)

The translation Shakespeare, the Puritans, and most pre-KJV English-speaking esotericists actually used. Distinctive features: the original marginal notes are themselves a Reformation commentary layer.

## Practical Working Stack

For a serious practitioner: **Alter** for the literary-Hebrew read and commentary altitude, **Sefaria** open in a browser tab for Hebrew + Rashi + Targum side-by-side while sitting at the altar, **Brenton or NETS** when the working calls for the Greek inheritance (especially anything touching Christian Hoodoo, where the LXX numbering is what the saints' tradition assumes), **Geneva 1599** when working a specifically Reformation/Puritan-American grimoire current or matching what Hoodoo lineage practitioners up through the early 20th century would have read aloud, and **11Q5** when you want to see the scroll the Essenes actually held — including David's Compositions, which numbers his psalms at 4,050 and frames the Psalter itself as a prophetic-magical corpus.
