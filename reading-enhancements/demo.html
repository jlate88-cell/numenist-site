The Reading of Jordan Ross Atkins

A SOUL MAP · DRAWN BY THE HEAVENS · COMBINED
Born March 15, 1991 · Flint, Genesee County, Michigan

Sect & The Two Lights

Jordan, you were born by DAY. Your Sun sits in your 7th house, above the horizon, which makes yours a diurnal chart, the first thing a Hellenistic astrologer reads. For you that means Jupiter is the sect benefic, your gentler helper; Mars is the in-sect malefic, the harder planet that still works largely in your favor; and Saturn is the out-of-sect malefic, the one whose lessons you earn the hard way.

Sun in Pisces 24°52' · 7th house. This is your conscious identity, vitality, and will. In Pisces it works fluidly, through compassion, imagination, and dissolving edges, and by essential dignity it draws on the visionary current of the late degrees.

Ascendant & Lord of the Geniture

Jordan, your Ascendant, the eastern horizon at your first breath, the most personal point in the entire chart, is Virgo rising at 18°27'. It sets your body, your manner, your whole life-arc, and the first impression you make: a Virgo surface that meets the world precisely, through craft, service, and discerning refinement.

The Lord of your Ascendant, the planet that rules Virgo and therefore steers the whole chart, is Mercury. This planet is you-as-protagonist; its condition is your operating budget for the whole life, so read it closely.

The Four Pillars (BaZi)

Jordan Ross Atkins, your Four Pillars are 辛未 · 辛卯 · 甲申 · 癸酉. Eight characters total · one Heavenly Stem above and one Earthly Branch below per pillar · inscribed at the moment of your first breath. Your Day Master 甲 Jiǎ (Yang Wood) is the central character; the other six characters are read as they relate to it.
